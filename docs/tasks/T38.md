## Objective and scope

Connect the T31 billing domain to T37 real usage. Let authorized administrators configure a versioned rate card; produce project-scoped, itemized charge statements and preserve history independent of current resource inventory.

**Dependencies:** T31 and T37.
**Mapped requirements:** Pricing units, periods, currency, rounding, stopped/deleted rules, project-level billing, persistent history, controlled calculation verification.

T31 provides a tested local billing service, immutable rate-card versions, monthly project statements, explicit provenance/completeness, role checks, and append-only revisions. `NovaBillingUsageSource` now verifies its refreshed project-scoped `billing_collector` Keystone session before querying Nova. It records when the API response was collected and the fixed reason codes for unverified active-state coverage, pagination and per-sample timestamps. The billing ledger preserves those fields, and CSV schema version 2 exports them. Batches remain incomplete, so statements can show observed itemized lines but have a blank total. The one-off operator collector still uses a protected administrator credential, not the dedicated collector identity required for deployment.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Administrators configure rates by metric/unit, amount, ISO currency, precision, effective UTC start/end, rounding rule, and resource state charging policy.
- [ ] Billing period is a UTC calendar month with `[start, end)` boundaries; a rate change does not rewrite a past statement.
- [ ] Initial compute charge uses provider-confirmed active vCPU-hours and active GiB-RAM-hours. Stopped/deleted compute stops at its provider-confirmed state transition; supported volumes charge by provisioned GiB-hour until confirmed deletion.
- [ ] Every statement groups usage by project and includes resource, metric, quantity, unit, rate version, amount, period, provenance, and completeness state.
- [ ] Only complete real samples can produce an `observed` statement. Incomplete coverage produces `provisional`; any synthetic source yields `simulated`.
- [ ] Prices are tested with controlled workloads and an independent Decimal calculation. No float conversion or silent unit mapping occurs.
- [ ] Resource deletion does not remove an issued statement. Corrections append a revision/adjustment.
- [ ] Statements are described as POC charge summaries, not tax invoices. No card, bank or payment data is accepted.
